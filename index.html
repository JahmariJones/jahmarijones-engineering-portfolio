Electromagnetic Generator

A motor-driven generator built to light an LED. A spinning magnet core induces voltage in an 800-turn copper coil, and a full bridge rectifier board feeds the LED.

  • Compared design concepts with a weighted Pugh matrix and combined a magnet-and-coil core with gear-driven speed multiplication.
  • Swapped the planned belt drive for a 20:10 spur gear pair, doubling rotor speed without slip.
  • Oriented the magnets perpendicular to the coil to maximize flux, following Faraday's law.
  • Soldered the rectifier PCB: four diodes in a bridge, a smoothing capacitor, and the LED with its current-limiting resistor.
  • Traced LED flicker on the scope to magnet shift, gear friction, and vibration, and documented fixes for a next revision.
Output
2.80 Vpp at 21.83 Hz
Coil
800 turns, 3D-printed former
Gearing
20 : 10 teeth, 2:1 speed-up
Tools
Fusion 360, 3D printing, soldering, oscilloscope
